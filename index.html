Our Story

Born from the embers of tradition

Sigri started as a single charcoal pit and a family recipe book. Today, every dish that leaves our kitchen still passes through the same open flame — nothing rushed, nothing faked.

We marinate overnight, grill slow, and let the smoke do what no sauce ever could.
